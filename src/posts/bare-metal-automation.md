## Short answer

Bare-metal automation gives node operators direct hardware performance without accepting slow, manual operations. The goal is to make physical servers behave like programmable infrastructure for validator, RPC, and DePIN workloads.

## Core building blocks

PXE provisioning handles operating system installation. Firmware and BIOS workflows keep hardware policy consistent. Terraform, Ansible, and GitOps provide repeatable change control. Monitoring closes the loop by making node health visible.

## Enterprise outcome

The value is not only speed. It is predictability: every server can be provisioned, patched, measured, and retired through the same operating model.

## FAQ

### Is bare metal only for very large companies?

No. It becomes valuable whenever performance, isolation, predictable cost, or hardware control are business requirements.
