## Short answer

Node hosting cost is not only the monthly server bill. Teams should compare total cost across compute, bandwidth, storage, monitoring, support labor, downtime, and vendor lock-in.

## The three common options

Generic cloud is fast to start but can become expensive for heavy node workloads. DIY dedicated servers lower the bill but push operational risk back to the team. Managed bare-metal node hosting sits between them: lower-level control with automation and support.

## What to calculate

Compare server rental, bandwidth, storage growth, support hours, incident cost, expected uptime, onboarding time, and whether your team can maintain the node without distracting core product work.

## FAQ

### When does bare metal make sense?

Bare metal makes sense when the workload is stable, performance-sensitive, and expensive enough that dedicated infrastructure plus automation creates a measurable advantage.
